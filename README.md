# Survey roadmap: paths through noisy-label learning

**Live page:** https://stormaref.github.io/survey-roadmap/

A reading map for a survey in progress on **learning with noisy labels**. Instead of a taxonomy of method
families, the survey follows the **paths** the field took: where each line of work started, and what each
paper added to it.

- Each lane is one research path. Time runs left to right.
- An arrow means *the later paper builds on the earlier one*. **Solid** arrows mean the citation was confirmed
  (OpenAlex reference lists or the paper's PDF). **Dashed** arrows are conceptual links where no citation was found.
- **Blue cards are papers I have read.** The page is updated by pushing to this repository.
- Click a paper to highlight its whole lineage and see what it added and what it left open.

The one-line summaries are drafts written with AI assistance and are checked as each paper is read.
Citation counts come from OpenAlex.

Maintained by Aref Azizian.
